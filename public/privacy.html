Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information EchoRooms collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using EchoRooms you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. This policy works alongside our Terms & Conditions.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: when you sign up, we collect your email address, display name, and a password (or Google account, if you choose that method).
  • Profile data: your username, status text, and avatar image, which you can add or edit.
  • Content you create: your messages, files, attachments, tasks, and other content posted to rooms.
  • Usage data: technical information such as your IP address, browser, device type, and how you interact with the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To deliver messages, files, and other content to the members of your rooms.
  • To provide features such as summaries, tasks, pinned messages, and search.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and investigate violations of our terms.
  • To improve, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

3. Data Processed by AI Features

When you request a room summary or extract tasks, the relevant messages from rooms you are a member of may be sent to our AI processing provider to generate that output. We only process content from rooms you can access, and we do not use your content to train AI models for unrelated purposes unless you give separate consent.

4. Third-Party Services

EchoRooms relies on service providers to operate, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and serverless functions.
  • Google for social sign-in, if you choose to use it.
  • AI providers for summary and task-extraction features.

These providers may process your data in accordance with their own policies. You can review Supabase's and Google's privacy policies for more details.

5. How We Store & Protect Data

Your data is stored with our hosting providers using industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest. Access controls and row-level security help ensure you can only see data from rooms you belong to. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal data in accordance with our deletion process. Copies distributed to other members of your rooms may remain where those members legitimately retain them.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. You can manage most profile information directly in your settings. To exercise other rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and session tokens to keep you signed in and to maintain the state of the app. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

EchoRooms is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the service or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us and we will direct your enquiry to the appropriate person.
