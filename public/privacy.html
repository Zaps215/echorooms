Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-17 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy describes the information processed by the current EchoRooms app and the choices available to you. It should be read with our Terms and Cookie Policy. By creating an account, you are asked to confirm that you have read and accept the current versions; the app records the account, policy versions, time, and sign-up method.

1. Information processed

  • Account and profile: email address, display name, username, and any status text or avatar you choose to add. Supabase Auth handles password credentials; EchoRooms does not store your password in its application tables. Google receives information as part of Google sign-in if you choose that option.
  • Private room data: room membership, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, delivery state, reactions, and other message metadata. The current client encrypts private message and attachment content before it is stored. Encryption does not hide metadata, protect a compromised device, or prevent a room member from copying content. Do not use the service for information requiring a guarantee of confidentiality.
  • Social content: feed posts, photos, comments, likes, and stories. Feed posts and their media are available to signed-in members. Stories may be limited to the audience selected when posted. Social content is not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Device data: the app stores a Supabase sign-in session and some app settings and encryption material in browser storage on your device. Browser notification permission is requested only if you enable it.
  • Connection data: Supabase and the hosting/provider services necessarily receive network information such as IP address, request time, and browser/device details when your browser connects. EchoRooms currently has no analytics SDK, advertising tracker, or in-app behavioral analytics.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, display profiles, deliver room messages and social content, store content you choose to share, provide calls and notifications you initiate, respond to support or legal requests, and operate and troubleshoot the service. The app does not currently contain AI summaries, task extraction, message search, or analytics features; private messages are not sent to an AI provider by the current app.

3. Service providers and external connections

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and realtime connections.
  • Google processes Google sign-in if selected. One-to-one calls also use Google's public STUN endpoint to help establish a peer-to-peer connection; the endpoint can receive network connection details such as your IP address.
  • The app uses no embedded third-party feed, video player, advertising widget, or analytics embed. It currently loads its fonts and icon locally and makes no Google Fonts or Flaticon asset requests.

Provider processing, infrastructure region, backups, and retention are subject to the provider configuration used for the deployment. Review each provider's policy for its own practices.

4. Sharing and visibility

Room content is available to room members. Room members can copy, capture, or otherwise disclose information they can view. Feed posts are visible to signed-in members, and story visibility follows the selected audience settings. Profile information is visible to signed-in users as described in the app. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Security

Private message and attachment content is encrypted by the client before storage; social posts and profile data are not end-to-end encrypted. Access controls are present in the checked-in database setup, but the active production project's configuration and hosting controls must be verified separately. No service can promise absolute security. Keep your device and account credentials secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and content data are retained while needed to operate the service. Account deletion triggers deletion of account-linked records, but other room members may have retained or copied information they could access, and provider backups may persist for a provider-defined period. Policy acceptance records are linked to the account and are removed when the account is deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit profile information, choose story audiences, delete your account, and control browser notifications and device permissions. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. The service currently has no dedicated privacy-request form; the operator must publish a working privacy contact before public launch.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The current app does not set its own browser cookies and has no optional analytics or advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage for the Supabase session, app preferences, local chat state, and device encryption material. These are used for core app functions. See our Cookie Policy. Because no optional tracking cookies are currently used, the app does not display an advertising/analytics cookie consent banner.

9. Children

EchoRooms is not intended for children under 13 or anyone below the minimum age required by local law. The current signup flow does not verify age. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information should contact the service operator; a working contact address must be published before public launch.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will update the version and effective date when we do. A material change should be presented for renewed acceptance before users continue to use the service. The operator's legal name, place of establishment, and privacy contact are not configured in this codebase and must be supplied before public launch. This policy is an operational draft, not legal advice; have qualified counsel review it for the jurisdictions where the service is offered.
