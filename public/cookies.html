Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Version 2026-10-17 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy describes browser cookies and storage used by the current EchoRooms app. Read it with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Cookies

The current EchoRooms app does not set its own cookies. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party tracking cookies, and it does not load an embedded advertising or analytics service. Google OAuth may use cookies on Google's own site during sign-in; those are controlled by Google.

Local and session storage

The app uses browser local storage for essential functions, including the Supabase sign-in session, appearance preference, local message-clearing state, and a device encryption secret. It uses session storage briefly to remember a pending Google signup consent while the browser completes the provider redirect. These values stay in your browser unless browser settings, extensions, or device synchronization copy them elsewhere.

Notifications and permissions

Browser notification permission is requested only after you choose to enable it. Camera, microphone, and photo-library access are requested only when you start the corresponding feature. These browser permissions are managed in your browser or device settings and are not cookies.

Your choices

You can clear site storage or block it in your browser settings, but doing so can sign you out, remove local preferences, or make encrypted room keys unavailable on this device. The service currently has no optional tracking technologies, so it does not display a separate analytics/advertising cookie-consent banner. If this changes, optional technologies must remain disabled until any consent required by applicable law is obtained.

Policy changes

We will update the version and date if the storage technologies used by the app change. The operator's legal identity and contact details must be published before public launch. This document is an operational draft, not legal advice.
