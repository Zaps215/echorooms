# EchoRooms Promo Video Prompt

## Objective
Create a clean, professional, social-first promo video for EchoRooms — a modern messaging and community app that blends private chat, social feed, stories, and real-time collaboration in a polished mobile-first experience.

The video should feel premium and believable, not overly AI-generated. Keep the tone warm, modern, and polished. Use smooth motion, subtle transitions, and a strong focus on clarity and trust.

## Brand direction
- Visual style: premium, minimalist, social app aesthetic
- Feel: clean, confident, polished, human, and trustworthy
- Look: Instagram/WhatsApp-inspired structure with a more refined product-brand feel
- Colors: soft grey backgrounds, clean white cards, blue brand accents, teal highlights, violet feed accents, and warm neutral tones
- Text: simple, modern, readable, minimal
- Motion: smooth, subtle, elegant; avoid flashy or gimmicky transitions

## Core message
EchoRooms is a social and messaging app where people stay connected, share moments, and continue private conversations without losing the human feel of everyday communication.

## Key features to highlight
- Social feed with posts, likes, comments, and stories
- Private chats with real-time messaging and status indicators
- Blue, premium chat UI with compact message bubbles and polished timestamps
- Safe, private, encrypted conversation feel
- Reactions, voice notes, and direct messaging experience
- Community feel with stories and recent conversations
- Mobile-first experience with clean navigation and professional layout

## Recommended structure

### Scene 1 — Intro / brand reveal (2–3 seconds)
- Start on a clean background with the EchoRooms logo and app name.
- Fade in with subtle motion and a soft product intro.
- Add a short headline: “Stay connected. Share moments. Keep conversations alive.”

### Scene 2 — Feed and stories (4–6 seconds)
- Show the Feed view with a stories strip and a polished post card.
- Highlight a warm, modern lifestyle post or profile moment.
- Animate into the stories section and then into the feed post card.
- Show likes, comments, and clean social-app interaction.

### Scene 3 — Community interaction (4–5 seconds)
- Show a recent chat list, story moments, and user conversations.
- Emphasize the “social feed meets private messaging” idea.
- Show the app as a place where people check updates and keep in touch naturally.

### Scene 4 — Chat experience (6–8 seconds)
- Open a polished chat screen with a contact header and active online status.
- Show a compact message thread with clean bubbles and timestamp styling.
- Highlight real-time conversation flow, straightforward replies, and message reactions.
- Keep the UI clean and premium, with no clutter.

### Scene 5 — Private and expressive messaging (5–7 seconds)
- Show a quick back-and-forth conversation with a message bubble and reaction state.
- Feature the casual, expressive chat feel: quick replies, reactions, direct communication.
- Show voice-note or message action element if available without overdoing it.

### Scene 6 — Closing CTA (2–4 seconds)
- End with the app’s core promise in bold but simple language.
- CTA example: “EchoRooms — where social moments meet real conversations.”
- Fade to a clean branded ending screen.

## Visual execution notes
- Keep transitions smooth and subtle.
- Avoid heavy stock footage or unrealistic motion.
- Prefer app UI shots with crisp framing and clean whitespace.
- Keep the app appearance close to the current product design: refined, social-first, and not overly futuristic.
- Use soft shadows, rounded corners, and premium spacing.
- Make the UI feel like a real polished product demo, not a mockup or heavy marketing graphic.

## Content tone
Use language like:
- “Stay connected”
- “Share your world”
- “Keep conversations alive”
- “Private chats. Social moments. One home.”

## Example promotional copy
EchoRooms brings together the feeling of a social app and the usefulness of a modern messenger — so you can share moments, stay in touch, and keep your conversations flowing in one place.

## Screenshot references to use
- Feed screen with stories and post layout
- Recent conversations or social feed overview
- Direct chat screen with modern conversation styling
- App navigation and profile layout

## Suggested final prompt for a video-generation tool
Create a polished promo video for EchoRooms, a modern social messaging app that blends community feed, stories, direct messaging, and private chat in one elegant experience. The video should feel premium, clean, and professional with Instagram/WhatsApp-inspired UI, soft neutral colors, blue and violet accents, minimalistic motion, and a crisp product-demo aesthetic. Show the app’s feed with stories and social cards, then transition into a modern chat screen with real-time conversation, polished timestamps, clean bubbles, and status indicators. Emphasize a social-first vibe, privacy, and seamless communication. Use subtle transitions, smooth motion, concise text overlays, and a premium mobile app feel. No clutter, no exaggerated effects, no heavy AI look. Final message: “EchoRooms — where social moments meet real conversations.”

## Notes
This is a promotional app demo, so the video should feel like a real product walkthrough rather than a technical explanation. Focus on the smooth emotional flow of the experience: seeing updates, chatting with friends, and staying connected in a polished environment.
